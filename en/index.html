What it is

Your Game Master second brain

Every campaign piles up names, places, secrets and promises made at the table. CriticalPrep keeps them in one place and helps you at three moments.

1

Prepare

Build the world with NPCs, places, factions, quests and deities. Import your notes and let the AI turn them into tidy entries.

2

Play

At the table, find any entry in a moment, ask the campaign who knows what and record the session, Discord included.

3

Update

After the session the AI reads notes and transcript and proposes changes to your entries. You choose which to keep, one by one.

FAQ

Questions

How much does it cost?

During the BETA CriticalPrep is free, AI included. You can also use a free local model or your OpenRouter account.

Which RPGs does it work with?

All of them: D&D, Pathfinder, Call of Cthulhu, homebrew systems. You set the system in the campaign details and the AI takes it into account.

Do I need an Internet connection?

Only for the included AI or cloud providers. With a local model (Ollama or LM Studio) and without Discord recording it also works offline.

Where is my data stored?

On your computer, in a folder you can choose. Entries are Markdown files readable with any editor or Obsidian. There's no account and no CriticalPrep cloud.

Can the AI change my entries on its own?

No. After the session it proposes changes and you choose which to apply. Every change can be undone.

How does Discord recording work?

You create your own Discord bot; the app guides you step by step. The bot joins the voice channel and records each player on a separate track; transcription runs on your computer.

Can I use the same campaign on Mac and Windows?

Yes: campaigns are folders of ordinary files. Copy them or keep them in a synced folder and open them on both systems.

Why does my system show a warning the first time?

The BETA isn't signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft certificate yet, so the system asks for confirmation the first time. The steps are above, in the Download section.
